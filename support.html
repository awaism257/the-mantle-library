About

The Mantle Library presents classical devotional works in praise of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ — the Dalāʾil al-Khayrāt of Imam al-Jazūlī, Kaʿb ibn Zuhayr's Banat Suʿād, Ṭalaʿa al-Badru ʿAlaynā, and poems of Ḥassān ibn Thābit — verse by verse in the Digital Khatt IndoPak script with English translation, alongside historic public-domain recordings from the earliest decades of the gramophone era, in light and dark themes.

The app works fully offline: all texts and fonts are bundled, and recordings are downloaded only when you choose to play them.

Texts & licences

Dalāʾil al-Khayrāt Imam al-Jazūlī (d. 1465) — classical text in the public domain, as circulated in standard editions. English: opening devotions, intention and Hizb 1 — original translation made for this project; Hizbs 2–8 — historical translation by Rev. John B. Pearson (Guide to Happiness, Oxford, 1907), in the public domain.
Banat Suʿād Kaʿb ibn Zuhayr — the recension transmitted in Ibn Hishām's Sīra (public domain). English: original translation made for this project.
Ṭalaʿa al-Badru ʿAlaynā The traditional text as transmitted in the sīra literature (public domain). English: original rendering made for this project.
Poems of Ḥassān ibn Thābit As transmitted in his dīwān and, for the minbar poem, in Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim (public domain). English: original renderings made for this project.
Recordings Public-domain 78rpm discs (1901–1921) — courtesy of the Harvard Loeb Music Library (Arabic 78 Collection), the Bibliothèque nationale de France (Gallica), and Excavated Shellac.
