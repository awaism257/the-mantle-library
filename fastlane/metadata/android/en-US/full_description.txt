Mantle Library is a free, ad-free, offline-first library of classical Islamic devotional works — featuring a complete 12-chapter narrated audiobook on the Seerah (the prophetic biography), classical qasidas, litanies, and prophetic supplications — presented in Arabic with scholarly English translations, and paired with 15 remastered historic gramophone recordings from the golden age of Islamic devotional art (1901–1921).

---

📖 The Library

The Life of the Prophet ﷺ (Al-Sīrah al-Nabawiyyah) — السيرة النبوية
A complete 12-chapter narrated audiobook on the blessed Seerah. Presented in continuous flowing English prose with live sentence-by-sentence recitation highlighting, Victorian source notes, and authentic Arabic transliteration.

Dalāʾil al-Khayrāt — دلائل الخيرات
The celebrated 15th-century collection of blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ by Imam Muḥammad al-Jazūlī. Presented in the public-domain English translation by Rev. John B. Pearson (Oxford, 1907), with the complete Arabic text and modern reading aids.

Mishkāt al-Maṣābīḥ — مشكاة المصابيح
Selected chapters of prophetic devotions from the classical hadith anthology by al-Khaṭīb al-Tibrīzī (14th century): morning and evening adhkār, night prayers, post-prayer supplications, and the Chapter on Seeking Forgiveness — presented with Arabic originals and the first English translation (Captain James Robson Matthews, Calcutta, 1809–1810).

Morning & Evening Adhkār — أذكار الصباح والمساء
The prophetic litanies of the day and night, drawn from Ḥiṣn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim), with Arabic text and English.

🎵 Historic Devotional Recordings

A unique collection of 15 public-domain 78 RPM recordings from the very dawn of commercial Arabic gramophone recording — digitally remastered to remove surface noise, clicks, and motor hum while preserving the warmth of the original shellac masters.

Artists and sessions include:
• Shaykh Yūsuf al-Manyalāwī — Gramophone Concert & Monarch Records, Cairo 1909–1910 (the master of the Khedivial golden era)
• Aḥmad al-Mīr — Baidaphon label, c.1907–1911
• Aḥmad al-Ajamī — Favorite Records, Cairo 1905
• Saʿīd al-Saftī — Favorite Records, Cairo 1905
• Al-Shaykh Aḥmad al-Shaykh — Baidaphon label
• Wadūdah al-Minyalāwī — Victor Records, Cairo 1915
• Alexis Ben Kori — Archives de la Parole, Sorbonne, Paris 1914
• Tatar Trio — E. Berliner's Gramophone, Kazan 1901 (among the oldest surviving Islamic devotional recordings)

All recordings pre-date 1926 and are fully in the public domain. Each disc is presented with its original archival label artwork — displayed as a spinning vinyl disc in the player.
